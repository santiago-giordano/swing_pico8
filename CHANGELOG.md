# Changelog

Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/). Versions follow [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [2.7.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- **Duplicate** with X+O. On a pattern grid (beats and keys) it copies the pattern into the next letter when that one is empty and moves there; if it is not empty nothing is overwritten. On the song page, on a block, it inserts a copy of the block right after it and shifts the rest.
- **fill** on the song page of swing_beats and swing_mix (song byte 1, bits 1–2): on the last bar of every block that repeats ×2 or more, an open hat on step 16, a snare roll on steps 13–16, or both. Recordings include it.
- **ref drm** on the song page of swing_keys (byte 239 bit 6): the song's drums play under the keys while you play. Off while recording. Keys notes are delayed by the PCM latency to stay with the drums (about ±2 ms against the drums in the test rig). Needs PCM.
- Tests for fill, both duplicate gestures and the drum reference.

### Changed
- The footer line starts one pixel to the left so 32-character help texts fit.

## [2.6.0] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed
- With **room** raised, stopping could leave a constant offset on the PCM output (fixed-point math never lets a negative tail reach 0). The drum engine now clears the room buffers once the voices have ended and the tail is gone, and the output is exact silence. Same fix in swing_mix. **crush** now rounds instead of flooring, which removes a small DC bias.
- Changing kit, tune or speaker on the kit page no longer re-synthesizes all five drums in one frame: the kick updates at once (to listen to it) and the other tracks follow one per frame.

### Added
- **speaker** option for the bass in swing_keys (sound page, last row of the bass column, on by default): bass notes below E3 (~160 Hz) go up an octave. Song byte 239 bit 5 (0 = on). In swing_mix the bass follows the mix page's speaker switch (byte 127 bit 5).
- Tests: room tail ends in exact silence (beats and mix, with and without crush); bass fundamental doubles with speaker on (keys and mix).

### Changed
- The drum engine skips its filter, saturation and room work while no voice is playing, which also lowers CPU in swing_beats and swing_mix between hits and when stopped.

## [2.5.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- **click** has a third value, *met*: a metronome on every beat while playing (accent on beat 1), in all four carts. It never sounds in recordings. The values are now *no* / *rec* (count-in when recording, the old *on*) / *met*.
- **speaker** switch on swing_mix's mix page. It is the swing_beats setting (song byte 127 bit 5), so changing it in either cart changes the kick for both.
- `test_metronome` in `tests/test_mix.py`: quarter-note clicks while playing with *met*, none with *rec*, in the four carts.

### Changed
- swing_mix shows the recording status on its own status row instead of the shared one at the bottom.

## [2.4.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added
- `swing_mix.p8`, a fourth cart to hear the whole song: it carries the three engines (drums and noise over PCM, keys on the chip channels) and plays the arrangement or loops a pattern. Per-stem level, mute and solo, stored in song bytes 14–15; meters; *Pause → record mix* with the count-in. If CPU stays above ~88 % the noise drops to half rate until the next stop.
- `tests/test_mix.py`: keys vs drums alignment inside the mix (within one frame), mute/solo, and record-mix count-in.
- swing_mix in the Knulli gamelists, images and video, the page contact sheets and the itch kit.

## [2.3.0] - 2026-10-18

### Fixed
- On a handheld speaker the kick was nearly inaudible (its body sits at 50–160 Hz) while the hat came out as the loudest hit, so the KIK and HAT rows seemed swapped. New **speaker** option on the kit page, on by default: the kick gets a short knock (700 → 300 Hz) and saturation. Above 400 Hz the kick now sits within about ±6 dB of the hat, up from 14–19 dB below.

### Changed
- CPU meter on the kit page moved down a row to make room for the new option.

## [2.2.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added
- Scraper media for Knulli / Batocera / ROCKNIX (EmulationStation): `knulli/gamelist.xml` (Spanish) and `knulli/gamelist.en.xml`, a screenshot and title image per cart in `knulli/images/` and a 16 s video with each cart's own stem in `knulli/videos/`. Generated by `tools/media.py` from the test rig.

### Verified
- First full run on an Anbernic RG CubeXX (Knulli, official PICO-8): PCM detected, steady playback at 90 BPM, CPU on the kit page about 35–40 % while playing (it reads 5 % on frames where the audio buffer is already full).

## [2.1.0] - 2026-10-18

### Fixed
- swing_beats crashed ("attempt to perform arithmetic on field '?'") when the kit or tune was changed while the pattern played: the new drum buffers could be shorter than a voice still sounding. Voices are now cut on re-synthesis. Found on the first run on an Anbernic RG CubeXX with official PICO-8.
- swing_keys could land notes 2–3 frames (33–50 ms) late: its clock followed the PCM buffer, which drains in blocks. The clock now advances one frame per frame and is nudged gently toward the PCM consumption, so notes stay within ±1 frame without drifting.

### Changed
- tone, drive and room are applied live and no longer re-synthesize the kit (no CPU spike, no click).
- `build.py` fails when a cart exceeds the token, character or (estimated) compressed-size limits.

### Added
- Rendered audio samples and a demo GIF in `docs/`.
- GitHub Actions: CI on every push, release with the carts zip on every `v*` tag.
- `tests/test_live_edit.py` and `tests/run_all.sh`.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added
- Dilla-style groove page in swing_beats:
  - per-track swing (or follow the song swing) and a 16th / 8th swing base;
  - 8 groove templates with per-step offsets in 1/48 of a step (they scale with tempo); tapping O applies the template's preset;
  - flam (ghost hit 10/20/30 ms before mid and hard hits);
  - phrase drift: swing grows 0–3% per bar and resets every 4 bars;
  - humanize modes: random, smooth (correlated AR(1)) and lock (same micro-offsets every bar).
- `tests/test_groove.py`.

### Changed
- The sequencer now runs on a straight grid and every event carries its own swing offset (no accumulated swung durations).
- Events are scheduled 100 ms ahead (was 42 ms), so hits can be pulled early.
- Nudge range is now −64…+60 ms in beats and keys (was ±40).
- swing_noise ducking follows the kick track's actual timing.

Songs made with 1.x open unchanged: the new fields default to "off".

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-18

First public version: swing_beats, swing_keys and swing_noise sharing a song file, MPC-style swing, per-track nudge, humanize, record mode with click count-in, Spanish/English UI and a headless test rig on patched fake-08.
